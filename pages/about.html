How It All Began

Bloopville was born in 2026 with a simple mission: to spark creativity, embrace curiosity, and inspire a sense of adventure in people of all ages.

Our business focuses on original character creation, storytelling, and translating those personalities into a world of merchandise - plush toys, electronics, clothing, household items, and more.

Far away in the cosmos, on the peaceful planet of Bloopia, lives a group of adorable creatures known as the Bloops. Their harmony was shattered when a meteorite crashed, draining their planet's vital energy - the JajaBooma. Now they have traveled to Earth in search of friendship, creativity, and a way to restore their home.

Every character you meet carries a piece of that story - and a piece of our hearts. Welcome to Bloopville.

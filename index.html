Explore Characters That Spark Joy

Frequently Asked

Everything you need to know about Bloopville.

What makes Bloopville characters special? +

Each Bloop is hand-designed with a unique personality, backstory, and superpower. We blend storytelling with premium craftsmanship to create characters you will truly love.

How long does shipping take? +

Standard shipping takes 3-5 business days within the US, and 7-14 days internationally. Express options are available at checkout.

Do you ship worldwide? +

Yes! We ship to over 40 countries. Shipping rates and delivery times are calculated at checkout based on your location.

What is your return policy? +

We offer a 30-day return policy on all unused items in original packaging. Just contact us and we will send you a prepaid return label.

Are the products safe for kids? +

Absolutely. All Bloopville products are tested and certified to meet or exceed international safety standards for children's products.
